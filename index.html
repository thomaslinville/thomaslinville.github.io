Motor Controller Discharge PCB

Washington SuperBike, 2025 to present

The motor controller had a critical shared HV/LV ground safety flaw. I designed a board in Altium that fixed it, then hand-assembled it and brought it up myself.

  • Replaced the MOSFET stage with a 600 V-peak-isolation optical relay.
  • Validated in live high-voltage testing: the 96 V bus discharged to under 2 V within 60 s.

Custom SmartWatch

4-layer PCB, layout in progress, targeting completion Winter 2026

A wearable built around an nRF5340 MCU with a 1.39-inch AMOLED display, designed in Altium. Everything has to fit on a 4-layer board sized for a wrist.

  • Power: 420 mAh LiPo through a protected nPM1300 power path split into 3 rails (1.8/3.0/3.3 V).
  • Sensors: BMI270 IMU, SAM-M10Q GPS, MAX30102 PPG, split across 2 I2C buses, with the 1.8 V pull-ups retuned from 4.7 kΩ to 3.3 kΩ for rise-time margin.
  • Prototyping on an nRF5340 DK with sensor breakouts and a current-monitored bench supply.
